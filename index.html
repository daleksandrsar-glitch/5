Technology: M.A.R.S. Companion

M.A.R.S. Companion is engineered as an autonomous physical device executing real-time voice processing and long-term semantic memory locally. The architecture originated from a core principle: your personal companion must remain your trusted ally without ever routing your thoughts to third-party corporate servers.

The system functions completely air-gapped, ensuring total data sovereignty and privacy by design.
